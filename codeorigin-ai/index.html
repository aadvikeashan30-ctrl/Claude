FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Code Origin.Ai train on my code?

No. Your code, prompts and outputs are never used to train models. Enterprise plans add zero-retention mode and customer-managed keys.

Which IDEs and tools does it integrate with?

Native plugins for VS Code, JetBrains, Neovim and Xcode, plus a CLI, GitHub/GitLab apps, Linear, Slack and Jira.

Can I bring my own LLM?

Yes — Enterprise customers can route Origin to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, AWS Bedrock, or self-hosted models behind their own gateway.

How is it different from a code-completion tool?

Completion tools predict the next token. Origin Agents plan multi-step work, run tests, browse your repo and open reviewed PRs — like a teammate, not autocomplete.

What if the agent gets it wrong?

Every change goes through your normal PR flow. Origin shows its plan, its diff and its tests — you stay in control, always.
